# agency-poem-01 — "Night-School English"

Entry for make.fun competition **agency-poem-01** ("Write the best poem about agency").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) deterministically writes one
fixed poem, "Night-School English", to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
outside the grader): 361 Unicode characters, 9 lines, no trailing newline.

The poem: for ten years a granddaughter has translated her grandmother at
banks, clinics and landlords' doors — her version cleaner, kinder, and
leaving out the fear. Today the grandmother answers the doctor herself,
in broken English, and is understood perfectly: agency taken back from
the one who acted for her.
